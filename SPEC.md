# Nova

## Who uses it
Owner-operator

## What they can do
- Best Practice integration tool.
- Automated/ AI staff rostering.
- Staff roster in calendar view (Main dashboard).
- Employee can flag what days they are unavailable in advance.
- Automatic staff notification for roster changes.
- Automatic roster adjustments for late notices.
- AI generates timesheet summary for each employee based on hours worked, standard pay and rates.

## Data
staffing: id, created_at

## Out of scope for now
- Automatic payslip generation.
- AI predicts workload and staffing based on past patterns.
- Highlight what days have the potential to be understaffed.
- Automatic leave management based on synced personal calendar.
